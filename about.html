How It Began

From a Pond to a
Purpose

"We didn't just want to farm. We wanted to show what good food from good land could look like."

Ankah Farms was born in Kotei Daduako from a simple belief — that the communities of Ghana deserve access to genuinely fresh, honestly raised food. We started with catfish because we knew it well, knew the demand, and knew we could do it right.

Every pond, every harvest, every fish delivered is a reflection of that original commitment. We raise our catfish in clean, well-managed water, fed properly, given space, and harvested at the right time.

This is just the beginning. Our land has more to offer — livestock, crops, and a farm that can serve our region for generations. For now, we do catfish and we do it with everything we have.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't just words on a wall. They're how we decide what to do and how to do it — every single day.

Honesty in Every Harvest

We don't cut corners. If a fish isn't ready, it doesn't ship. Quality means waiting for it to be right, not rushing it out the door.

Respect for Nature

We farm with the land, not against it. Clean water, natural feed, responsible management — because a healthy environment makes healthy food.

Community First

Our farm is in Kotei Daduako. Our customers are our neighbours. We exist to serve our community and contribute to the local economy.

Steady Growth

We are growing — but thoughtfully. Each new venture will be done the Ankah way: carefully, honestly, and only when we're ready to do it well.
